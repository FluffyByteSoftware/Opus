TCP: the login door

AddressHostArrivedAgoWhere
Recent is the newest five connections that reached the login door; Historical is every one since START SERVER (past ten thousand, the oldest finished ones go). Newest first, by address only: who logged in as what is in the log, on purpose. Host is the address's name from reverse DNS, when it has one. A finished connection is greyed and says how it ended. The three dots on a row open its menu: KICK closes an open connection where it stands (the client sees the connection drop and nothing else), and the other two put the row's address on the whitelist or the blacklist. Adding to the blacklist while the blacklist is on is a ban: every connection and player from that address is dropped then and there, the player with a Kicked.

UDP: the world

AddressAccountConnectedPlaying for Quiet for
Every player in the world over UDP, newest first: their address, the account they logged in as, when their Connect was accepted, how long they've been in, and how long since their last packet (a client sends a keep-alive every second, so this stays at 0 or 1 unless something is wrong). The character they're playing goes here once there are characters.

Addresses and ranges let in at the login door, while the whitelist is on.

Whitelist

Address or range
One address (1.2.3.4, or an IPv6 one) or one range (1.2.3.0/24) a line, in the order they were added. A change takes at once, on the next connection, and is written to the whitelist file in Content/cfg the same moment. While the whitelist is on, taking an entry away drops anybody online from an address no other entry covers, then and there. Whether the door looks at this list at all is access_list in networking.cfg, on the Settings tab; that switch takes on the next START SERVER. With the whitelist on and empty, nobody can log in.
Access list
--
--
On the blacklist
--
Addresses and ranges closed at the login door, while the blacklist is on.

Blacklist

Address or range
One address (1.2.3.4, or an IPv6 one) or one range (1.2.3.0/24) a line, in the order they were added. A change takes at once, on the next connection, and is written to the blacklist file in Content/cfg the same moment. While the blacklist is on, adding an entry is a ban: every open connection and every player from inside it is dropped then and there (a player hears a Kicked, reason banned). Whether the door looks at this list at all is access_list in networking.cfg, on the Settings tab; that switch takes on the next START SERVER.
